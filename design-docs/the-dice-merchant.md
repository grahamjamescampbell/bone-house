# The Dice Merchant

## Summary

The Dice Merchant is a merchant in the game Bone House. The Dice Merchant provides the opportunity for the player to purchase dice and other items to add to their inventory in order to benefit their play.

## Introducing The Dice Merchant

The Dice Merchant is introduced the first time the player busts from a table, most likely their first ever game.

The game is currently weighted to allow the player to amass two currencies quickly: Gold and Favors. Gold is twice as likely as Favor to show up. This means the player should quickly be able to purchase items from The Dice Merchant for Gold.

## The Inventory

The Dice Merchant hides his inventory within his coat. He opens his coat to displays his wares.

Items for purchase with Gold are displayed on the left.

Items for purchase with Favor are displayed on the right.

Viewing the Dice Merchant's inventory also gives the player a view of the Merchant's torso. It is clear the Merchant is not quite human, showing bone or squirming creatures, or just a dark void of infinite nothing, or a small fully inhabited town, or a small galaxy.

The player should not be distracted by the torso when viewing the inventory, but it should definitely be a point of interest. Something the player would mention to another player or call their partner over to "look at this weird thing".

### Items purchased for Gold

These are other dice.

The inventory refreshes throughout the game, but to start with the player can for the most part purchase only more of the standard dice already available at the start of the game.

The player will not be able use more than the basic 5 starting dice unless they purchase some of the Favor items that allow for more dice to be rolled.

The player will be able to choose which dice to use when sitting a new table, locking in their loadout of dice for the rest of that sit at that table.

### Items purchased for Favor

These are boons or materials and tools needed to augment dice.

They are very expensive and not immediately available to the player.

The player must purchase some of these items in order to create new dice of their own or swap the faces of their dice to those found on other dice to create a custom die.
